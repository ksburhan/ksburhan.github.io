# Lockpick: Support

Found a bug, stuck on something, or have a question? Write to
**[lockpick@ksburhan.com](mailto:lockpick@ksburhan.com)**. It helps to mention your phone model and the level
you were on.

## Common questions

**How do I get boosters?**
Clearing a level for the first time fills the reward meter: harder levels, a clean pick (no strain on the pick)
and solving within par fill it faster. A full meter gives you a random booster. From level 30 you can also watch a
short ad for a booster you've run out of, once per booster on each level.

**My pick snapped. What now?**
Use a Restore to keep your progress on the lock, or start over. Starting over and leaving a lock never cost
anything.

**How do I change ad or data choices?**
In the game, tap **Options** on the home screen. "Share play data" turns the anonymous play statistics on or off.
In the EU, UK and Switzerland, "Privacy choices" reopens Google's ad consent form. On iPhone, ad tracking can also
be changed in **Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking**.

**Will my progress carry over to a new phone?**
Your progress is saved on your phone only. It can move to a new phone when you restore the new phone from a backup
of the old one.

**How do I delete my data?**
Deleting the game deletes everything it saved on your phone. For the anonymous play statistics, see the
[privacy policy](../privacy/).
